About Me

I bridge the gap between rigorous design systems and robust engineering.

My approach is rooted in reductionism—stripping away the non-essential so that typography, layout, and interaction shine. Whether architecting a design system for enterprise software or building high-performance e-commerce experiences, I prioritize user intent and seamless execution.

When I'm not pushing pixels or writing clean TypeScript, you'll find me exploring analogue photography, reading architectural theory, or contributing to open-source UI libraries.
